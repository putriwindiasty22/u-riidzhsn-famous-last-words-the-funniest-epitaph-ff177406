## The entry

`main.ts` writes a single one-line tombstone epitaph — famous last words of
at most 150 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Here lies a man who said he'd sleep when he was dead. He kept his word.

He spent his whole life putting rest off until later. Death finally gave him
the sleep he'd been promising himself — and, annoyingly for everyone who knew
him, he kept his word.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the epitaph to `/output/entry.txt`.
